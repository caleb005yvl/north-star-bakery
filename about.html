How We Got Started

We started North Star Bakery with a pretty straightforward goal: bring a community-centered bakery back to this neighborhood. We wanted to build a spot where local families could stop in for a chat and walk out with bread that actually takes time to make. No shortcuts, just hard work.

Where We Get Our Ingredients

Good bread starts at the root. We source our stone-ground flour straight from regional heritage mills. By staying in touch with regional agricultural farmers, we keep our ingredients transparent, completely unbleached, and free from any weird artificial shelf-stabilizers.
